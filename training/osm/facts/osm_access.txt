# First three questions are training phrasings; the final phrasing is held out.
what does access mean in osm | what does the access tag describe | who may legally use this path | is access about legal permission => Access tags describe permission to use a feature.
which tag controls access on foot | how do i tag pedestrian permission | where do i record pedestrian access rights | what key is for walking access => Use foot to describe pedestrian access.
how do i ban motor vehicles in osm | tag no motor vehicle access | cars and motorcycles are forbidden here | which tag forbids motor vehicles only => Use motor_vehicle=no; check other modes separately.
can foot override general access | does a specific access tag take priority | walking is allowed despite a general access ban | what overrides access=no for walking => A specific tag such as foot=yes overrides the general access value.
is access=yes proof a road is passable | does permission mean physical access | can a permitted route still be impassable | can legal access guarantee a usable road => No. Legal permission and physical usability are different.
